The Paramedical Staff Association has protested the conduct of security personnel at Turbat Teaching Hospital, saying that during a VIP visit by Islamabad-based YouTubers, all routes into the hospital were sealed and doctors, paramedics, and nursing staff were barred from entering — and were treated, they say, with abusive and disrespectful language.

According to PMA and paramedic sources, staff at the Teaching Hospital have continued their strike and protest, boycotting medical services in objection to being denied entry while YouTubers from Islamabad and other cities were escorted in.

The episode points to a recurring grievance in Balochistan: the priority given to managed, high-profile visits over the ordinary functioning of public institutions. In sealing a working hospital for a guest tour, the staff say, the authorities placed a public-relations exercise ahead of patient care — leaving the very people who run the hospital locked out of it.

The association's demand is straightforward: an explanation for the treatment of hospital staff, and an assurance that access to the hospital — for those who work there and the patients who depend on it — will not again be suspended for the sake of a visit.
