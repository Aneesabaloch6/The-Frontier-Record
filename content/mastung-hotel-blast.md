Unidentified armed men reportedly targeted the Madina Hotel with explosives in the Reki Boz area of Khadkocha tehsil, Mastung, on Monday evening, completely destroying the hotel building.

According to local sources, the blast also destroyed an adjacent retail shop and damaged around 250 solar panels installed to power nearby tube wells.

The motive behind the incident remains unclear, and the local administration had yet to issue a statement at the time of reporting.
