According to local accounts, a Pakistani quadcopter strike killed two Pashtun youths and wounded more than fifteen people. Among the dead and injured were young men, white-bearded elders, and other civilians.

In the hospital corridors, the sounds of groans and grief filled the air — the cries of mothers, the tears of children, and the silent eyes of the elderly.

Ten of the wounded were taken to Bannu DHQ Hospital. One of them, Malik, a pump clerk, was referred to Peshawar, and Gul Nawaz was transferred to KGN Hospital. The remaining wounded are being treated at Bannu DHQ Hospital.

## Martyrs

1. Muhammad, son of Hazrat Umar — age 17
2. Wasid Hafiz, son of Gulawas — age 20
