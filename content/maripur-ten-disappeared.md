Baloch Voice for Justice has condemned the enforced disappearance of ten Baloch individuals from Maripur, Karachi, during separate operations over the past week. Among them is Khalid Baloch, brother of former BSAC chairman Shabbir Baloch.

## The disappeared

1. Khalid Baloch
2. Sooraj
3. Shahbaz
4. Sakhi
5. Aamir
6. Sameer
7. Allah Bakhsh
8. Aamir Wahid
9. Aziz
10. Noor Dad Sabir Aziz

## A month of nighttime operations

For the past month, according to Baloch Voice for Justice, Rangers and intelligence personnel have carried out nighttime operations across several Baloch-majority areas of Karachi. Neighbourhoods have been cordoned off, homes searched, and residents detained.

Baloch Voice for Justice demands the immediate disclosure of the whereabouts of all ten forcibly disappeared individuals, and their release.
